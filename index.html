Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining Markaz Academy.

We provide programs tailored for young learners starting from Grade 4 (approx. 9 years old) up through high school, O/A-Levels, and pre-university levels. Courses are segmented by both age and skill proficiency.

We offer both! Our state-of-the-art campus provides hands-on laboratory environments, while our hybrid and live online sessions cater to remote learners with full interactive screen-sharing and code sandboxes.
